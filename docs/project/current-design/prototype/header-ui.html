Choosing the right inspection solution for your line

Start with the product, its packaging and the questions your production team needs to resolve.

IN THIS ARTICLE

Your productYour packagingYour lineDiscuss your application

Start with your product.

Tell us what you produce, the product format and what you want to discuss. If you already have a sample or a product description, include it in the conversation.

Describe the packaging.

Share the package material and dimensions when available. Keep any unconfirmed information marked for discussion rather than treating it as a final requirement.

Final suitability and configuration need to be confirmed for your product, packaging and line conditions.

Bring the line requirements together.

  • Product and package description
  • Line layout and available space, when known
  • Your inspection question and current process

These details provide a starting point for a discussion. They do not replace a confirmed technical proposal.
